# antislop audit 001 (2026-10-31)

Target: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js` on branch `claude/sleepy-bell-ynynj5` (commit after the first clean-up pass).
Mode: after. Nothing below has been changed yet. Pick the numbers you want fixed.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Lock = LOW.

## How it was checked

- Rendered in Chromium at 360, 768 and 1280 px wide; measured every link's size, horizontal overflow, cursor, and the first Tab focus.
- Contrast computed with the antislop-human `contrast-check.py` on composited background colors.
- Console: no errors at any width.

## Findings

### HIGH

1. **No design direction (R-37).** The repo has no `DESIGN.md` and no stated brand direction, so the current look (dark navy, teal accent, glass panels) is the model's default taste, not a choice you made. Until direction exists, any visual fix is a draft.
2. **Tap targets under 44 px (R-03).** Nav links are 42 px tall at every width. The email and phone links in the hero details card are 22 px tall (`amirulamira273@gmail.com` 210x22, `013-9298065` 101x22).
3. **Mobile nav breaks (R-03).** At 720 px and below the nav stacks under the brand and wraps, leaving "Contact" alone on a second line.
4. **No active state on controls (R-39).** Buttons and nav links have hover styles but no `:active` style, and the focus ring is the browser default rather than one designed for the dark background (it is visible in Chromium, so this is about the missing active state).
5. **Hero image is unoptimised (R-40).** The portrait `<img>` has no `width`/`height` and no `fetchpriority="high"`. `gambar.jpg` is 1919x2560 and 547 KB but is shown at 240 px.

### MEDIUM

6. **Default glow and gradients (R-01).** Teal and blue radial glows on the page background, plus gradients on the "AH" mark, portrait ring, details card and timeline line. None has a written purpose.
7. **Glass on too many surfaces (R-10).** `backdrop-filter: blur` is on the navbar, the hero details card and all four sections (6 surfaces; the cap is 1-2).
8. **Every surface floats (R-12).** Large shadows on the nav, portrait, details card, every section and every card, so nothing reads as elevated.
9. **Typeface and labels by default (R-06).** Space Grotesk is one of the listed AI-default display faces and was not chosen for a stated reason. Uppercase labels with wide tracking: the hero eyebrow (0.18em), "DETAILS", and the timeline dates.
10. **Template motion (R-19).** Every section uses the same fade-up on scroll, and every button and nav link lifts on hover. No motion level was ever chosen and nothing records why the page moves.

### LOW

11. **Uniform section rhythm (R-05).** About, Education, Skills and Contact are the same bordered dark panel with an H2 and a card grid. Only the content changes.
12. **Dark theme without a reason (R-21).** The page is dark by default with no stated reason and no light option.
13. **Accent overuse and palette spread (R-29).** Teal, blue and orange accents on a dark base. Teal appears on the eyebrow, buttons, bullets, timeline dots, borders and glows, so it no longer marks one key moment.
14. **No written reasons (R-31).** None of the major visual decisions (color, type, layout, cards, motion) has a one-line reason.
15. **Generic identity (R-20).** Swap the name and photo and the page could be anyone's dark portfolio template. Nothing visual is specific to you.
16. **Phone link has no country code (C-2).** `tel:0139298065` dials correctly from a Malaysian line, but from abroad it fails. `tel:+60139298065` works everywhere.

## Passed

- Contrast (R-25): every text pairing passes AA; the lowest is the orange "DETAILS" label at 7.89:1.
- No em dashes, fake statistics, testimonials or buzzwords (R-02, R-16, R-17, R-18).
- Every nav link and button has a real destination; `resume.pdf` exists (R-24, R-26).
- No horizontal overflow at 360, 768 or 1280 px (R-03).
- Reduced-motion is respected and content is visible without JavaScript (R-39).
- No code comments to clean up (antislop-code).
